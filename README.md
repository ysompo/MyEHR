# MyEHR

A private medical file for you and your family, pulled from your **Meuhedet** account.

- Lab results with graphs over time, medications, visits and documents for each family member.
- Ask free questions such as "what is my last hemoglobin?" (optional, answered by Claude with your own API key).
- Data stays **only on your device**, encrypted. There is no server, no tracking and no ads.
- You always log in on Meuhedet's own page. The app never sees or stores your password or SMS code.

Runs on **Android** phones and on **Windows / Mac** computers.
